Frequently asked questions

What is a telescopic expansion joint?

A telescopic (slip-type) expansion joint consists of a sleeve that slides inside an outer barrel to absorb axial pipe movement. Graphite or PTFE packing seals the annular gap, making it suitable for steam, hot-water and process piping where axial traverse is large.

What size range of telescopic expansion joints do you manufacture?

Size range and traverse data are pending confirmation from engineering. Contact Precise Engineers with your line size, operating pressure, temperature and required traverse for a project-specific quotation.

What sealing systems are available?

Graphite packing, PTFE packing and live-loaded seal arrangements are available depending on temperature and cyclic-service requirements. Live-loaded seals maintain constant packing compression as the bellows cycles.

What design code governs telescopic expansion joints?

ASME B31.3 Process Piping governs the piping-stress analysis and pressure-containment design of telescopic expansion joints supplied by Precise Engineers.

Which industries use telescopic expansion joints?

Steam distribution, district heating, hot-oil piping and process plants in refining and petrochemical service are the primary applications. Precise Engineers is an EIL-approved unit, ISO 9001:2015 certified, established 1994.
