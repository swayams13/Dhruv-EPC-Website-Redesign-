Company

Expansion joints from Anand since 1994.

Precise Engineers was established at Vitthal Udyognagar GIDC, Anand, Gujarat, in 1994. The works designs and manufactures metallic, rubber and fabric expansion joints to EJMA — circular 80 to 8,000 mm NB — as an EIL approved unit with an ISO 9001:2015 quality system, serving twelve sectors from oil & gas to the Department of Atomic Energy.
