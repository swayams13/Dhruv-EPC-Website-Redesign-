Frequently asked questions

What is a dismantling joint?

A dismantling joint is a flanged fitting with an adjustable length that allows valves, pumps and other in-line equipment to be removed for maintenance without cutting the pipe. The joint is compressed on installation and extended to create the clearance needed for removal.

What adjustment length is available?

Adjustment length data are pending confirmation from engineering. Precise Engineers sizes the dismantling joint to match the face-to-face dimension of the valve or equipment it serves.

Which flange standards do dismantling joints suit?

Dismantling joints are available drilled to ANSI B16.1, BS 4504 (EN 1092) and DIN 2501 flanges. Mixed drilling is available on request.

What seal materials are available?

EPDM for water and mild chemicals, NBR for oil-containing services and Neoprene for weather-exposed installations. The seal grade is selected to suit the medium and operating temperature.

What pressure ratings are offered?

Pressure capability data are pending from engineering. Precise Engineers is ISO 9001:2015 certified and EIL approved, supplying dismantling joints to water utilities, pumping stations and process plant across India.
