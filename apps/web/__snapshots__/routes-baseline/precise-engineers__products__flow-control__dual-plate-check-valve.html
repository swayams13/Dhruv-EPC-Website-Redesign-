Types & configurations

Wafer type

Sandwiched between flanges; lightest and most compact for in-line non-return duty.

Lug type

Threaded lugs allow end-of-line use or removal without disturbing adjacent piping.

Metal-seated

For high-temperature, high-pressure or erosive service where soft seats are unsuitable.

Soft-seated

PTFE or Buna-N seating for tight shut-off on clean services.

Frequently asked questions

What is a dual plate check valve?

A dual plate check valve uses two spring-loaded semicircular plates hinged on a central pin to allow forward flow and close rapidly against reverse flow. The split-plate design halves closing travel versus a single-disc swing check, greatly reducing water-hammer severity on pump shut-off.

What are the advantages over a swing check valve?

Dual plate check valves are shorter and lighter (API 594 face-to-face is far less than swing-check equivalents), close faster to limit reverse-flow surge, and can be installed in any orientation — horizontal, vertical upward or vertical downward.

What pressure classes are available?

ASME Class 150, 300 and 600. Engineering data are pending; contact Precise Engineers with your line pressure, temperature and medium for the correct body and trim selection.

Which standard governs dual plate check valves?

Design, testing and dimensions to API 594. Face-to-face per ASME B16.10. Pressure-temperature ratings per ASME B16.34.

What body and trim materials are available?

Body in WCB, CF8M, CF8, LCC or WC6. Plates in SS 316 or SS 304; springs in SS 316 or Inconel 625 for high-temperature service. Precise Engineers is ISO 9001:2015 certified and EIL approved.
